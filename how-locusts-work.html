Desert Locusts

A Desert Locust is a type of grasshopper; indeed, all types of locusts are grasshoppers, whilst not all grasshoppers are locusts. Desert Locusts fall under the category of Locusts, which is only about 20 of the 11-18 thousand species of grasshoppers that have been discovered. Even fewer, only about 10 species of grasshoppers cause mass agricultural damage, and the most damage-inducing of them all are Desert Locusts.

Desert Locusts have 2 different "phases", one is solitary whilst the other is gregarious. During their solitary phase, desert locusts tend to have cryptic colouration, larger eyes and antennae, a restricted diet, and, most importantly, live in very low-density populations, usually less than 3 per 100m^2.

During their gregarious phase, desert locusts become much more active. They have aposematic colouration, groom frequently, and have a much broader diet than they do when solitary. However, the key detail about this phase is that desert locusts will swarm in groups of more than 50 million locusts per km^2. This is what makes desert locusts so dangerous, as gigantic groups like these can easily devour hundreds of metric tons of crops in just a day.

Desert Locusts gather in huge swarms and then start to raid gigantic areas and devour everything in their path. This includes crops as well, which can devastate entire agricultural regions and trigger severe food shortages. This dramatic shift from a harmless insect to a destructive swarm is driven by environmental triggers. When heavy rains cause vegetation to grow in deserted areas, locusts crowd together to feed. This forced physical contact releases serotonin in their nervous system, triggering the rapid changes - physical, behavioural, etc - that transform them into their gregarious phase within just a couple of hours.

Desert locusts can eat almost 2 grams of food every day, which, when you consider the fact that there are hundreds of thousands of locusts in a single swarm, can be quite deadly. While it might seem small, 2 grams is an absurd amount - it is almost the weight of their bodies themselves. This increased hunger occurs from baby, dormant locusts being trapped inside the ground for more than 20 years.
