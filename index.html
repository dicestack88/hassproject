01 / The problem

Why are desert locusts so dangerous?

The desert locust is one of the most damaging natural disasters to human food security. However what makes the desert locust truly deadly is the biome in which it resides; the desert. Deserts are formed from air currents that push immensely dry air into areas roughly 30 degrees surrounding the equator. This is the reason deserts very rarely experience rain and as such deserts have very little agricultural capability. The area we will be focusing on for this presentation which is north africa and the middle east are mostly desert with some grasslands mixed in. This means the area has little agricultural capability and as such is more prone to experiencing food insecurity as can be seen in fig 1 below.

Because this region already experiences heightened levels of food insecurity one can imagine the devastation desert locusts add to the issue. With desert locust distribution and actual desert area sharing the same immense strip of land that stretches from Morocco to Pakistan this devastation is widely shared. So how much damage do desert locusts actually cause? and how can we stop them?
